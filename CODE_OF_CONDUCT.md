# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Zara a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or insulting comments
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **arafatsohan2003@gmail.com**. Maintainers will review and take appropriate action, which may include removing contributions or banning contributors.
